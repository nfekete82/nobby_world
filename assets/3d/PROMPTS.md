# Built-in ImageGen prompt set

The following prompts were used with built-in ImageGen. No API-key fallback was
used. `materials.png` and `foliage.png` are project-bound generation outputs; texture
quadrants are sampled at their actual dimensions by the renderer. The UI source
is normalized to a transparent 4×4 runtime atlas.

Production albedo texture atlas for an original lush stylized 3D fantasy action RPG
with warm cheerful hand-painted miniature-world art. ONE square1024x1024 opaque
image arranged strict2x2 equal512px material quadrants, all top down flat, NO
perspective NO lighting cast shadows NO scenery NO borders NO labels. Upper left:
lush clover and short meadow grass in #629946 #84b957 #477843, soft broad irregular
leafy patches with restrained tiny blades and occasional tiny buttercup specks,
low contrast organic texture. Upper right: warm natural compact dirt path in
#b69a70 #c9ae80 #9a815e with broad irregular packed-earth patches and sparse tiny
pebbles; no grass. Bottom left: warm rough brown tree bark #795136 #a3784d with
vertical organic knots and woody grain. Bottom right: soft weathered cream-gray
stone #a4ac97 #c6c7ac #7e897c organic mottled surface. Seamlessly repeatable spatially
homogeneous material surfaces. High quality handpainted albedo game textures,
softly sculpted shape detail, NO pixel art NO photorealism NO vignette NO outlines NO grid.
